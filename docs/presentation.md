# Portfolio presentation

The case introduces the central decision: a better reason to return must also create sustainable value. Its cover compares one credit grant with five smaller grants. This is a policy illustration, not a graph of experimental results.

## Visual direction

Paper `#f3f3eb`, charcoal `#202623`, muted green `#5e6a60` and lime `#d9f27e`. Manrope is hosted locally with Latin and Cyrillic subsets under the SIL Open Font License. The dark policy panel contrasts with the light analytical narrative. Numbered chapters, a restrained table of contents and highlighted interpretation notes guide the reader through the evidence.

The English case retains the full analytical narrative and dynamic results renderer. The Russian case offers a concise reading of the same documented findings. The D7 expiry boundary and economic uncertainty remain visible. No model result is represented as an achieved company outcome. The simulation, analytical calculations, dashboard and independent UCI research are unchanged by this presentation work.
